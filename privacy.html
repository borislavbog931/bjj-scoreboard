Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

BJJ Scoreboard is a scoring and timing tool for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu matches.

Data collection

BJJ Scoreboard does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, and no advertising.

Data storage

Fighter names, scores, and match settings are stored only in your device's local storage, for your convenience between sessions. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Permissions

The app may request screen-wake and vibration permissions, used only to keep the screen on during a match and to give haptic feedback on scoring actions and time signals. Neither permission is used to collect or transmit data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact details listed on the Google Play store page for this app.
